Privacy

ClearFeed is a personal Android application. It requests no runtime permissions and makes no network requests — the app has no internet access at all. The download website does not require an account or database.

How the blocker works

ClearFeed uses Android's Accessibility Service API to watch the real YouTube app for its Shorts player. When it opens, ClearFeed sends you back to your home screen. It reads only structural view identifiers from the screen — resource ids and view class names — never on-screen text, video titles, captions, comments, or account information. Nothing it reads is ever transmitted anywhere; it never leaves the device.

Activity log

ClearFeed keeps a short, in-memory log of its own recent activity (visible under "View activity" in the app) so a failure can be diagnosed from what you choose to share, not to send anything automatically. The log is never transmitted on its own and clears when the app closes.

Third-party services

YouTube is a third-party app with its own privacy policy. ClearFeed is not affiliated with Google.
