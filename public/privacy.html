Privacy

ClearFeed is a personal Android application. Sign-in credentials and website sessions remain inside Android System WebView on your device. The download website does not require an account or database.

Diagnostics

ClearFeed sends privacy-filtered technical events to the owner's Vercel runtime logs to diagnose loading failures. Events contain the app, Android and WebView versions, service name, diagnostic code, a shortened location without query parameters, and a bounded technical description. Passwords, cookies, authentication tokens, messages, media, full URLs and page contents are not collected.

Third-party services

Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube are third-party websites with their own privacy policies. ClearFeed is not affiliated with Meta or Google.
