Privacy

ClearFeed is a personal Android application. Sign-in credentials and website sessions remain inside Android System WebView on your device. The download website does not require an account or database.

Diagnostics

ClearFeed records technical diagnostics on your device so you can read them under More options → Diagnostics. Nothing is transmitted unless you switch on Send failure reports, which is off by default.

While that switch is on, ClearFeed sends privacy-filtered failure events — and only failure events — to the owner's Vercel runtime logs to diagnose loading problems. Normal navigation stages are never transmitted. A transmitted event contains the app, Android and WebView versions, the service name, a diagnostic code, a shortened location without query parameters or fragments, a bounded technical description, and a random identifier for the current app session. Passwords, cookies, authentication tokens, messages, media, full URLs and page contents are not collected. As with any network request, the receiving server also sees your IP address; turning the switch off prevents the request entirely.

Third-party services

Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube are third-party websites with their own privacy policies. ClearFeed is not affiliated with Meta or Google.
