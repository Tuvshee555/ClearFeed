Privacy

ClearFeed is a personal Android application. Sign-in credentials and website sessions remain inside Android System WebView on your device. The static download website does not require an account, database, or environment variables.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics are stored locally on the device and are only shared when you explicitly copy or export them. ClearFeed does not automatically upload them to this website.

Third-party services

Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube are third-party websites with their own privacy policies. ClearFeed is not affiliated with Meta or Google.
